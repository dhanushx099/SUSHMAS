About Our Clinic

At Dr. Sushma’s Skin Clinic, we understand that every patient’s skin and hair concerns are unique. We focus on understanding your concerns and recommending suitable treatment options based on your individual needs.

Whether you are concerned about acne, pigmentation, hair loss or skin ageing, we aim to help you make informed decisions about your skin health and appearance.

Every consultation begins with listening. We take the time to understand your concerns, your skin and hair type and what you hope to achieve, so the advice you receive is relevant to you rather than generic.

From there, we assess your condition clinically and explain the options that may suit you, including what to expect, the costs involved and any potential risks. Treatments are recommended only when they are clinically appropriate, and results can vary from person to person.

Our care brings together dermatology and cosmetology: acne and acne scars, melasma, pigmentation and tanning, hair and scalp concerns, eczema and skin allergies, and skin rejuvenation with peels and microneedling where indicated. We also guide you on everyday skin care and sun protection, and review your progress at follow-up visits so your plan can be adjusted when needed.
