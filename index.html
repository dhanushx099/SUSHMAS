Our services

Skin, hair and aesthetic care built around you

Acne and acne scar management

01.

We assess acne and acne scarring and recommend suitable management based on the clinical findings.

Melasma, pigmentation and tanning

02.

We assess melasma, uneven skin tone and tanning concerns, and consider selected treatments for pigmentation based on skin type and severity. Results vary, and ongoing sun protection is important.

Hair and scalp care

03.

We assess hair and scalp disorders and recommend suitable management, with hair restoration treatments where appropriate.

Peels, microneedling and skin rejuvenation

04.

Chemical peels, microneedling where indicated and other rejuvenation procedures, offered according to clinical assessment and suitability.

Eczema and skin allergies

05.

We assess eczema, other inflammatory skin conditions and skin allergy concerns, and recommend management based on the clinical findings.

Skin consultations

06.

General and personalised skin-care consultations, with clear explanations of your treatment options and aftercare.
